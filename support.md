### ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**1. How do I delete a habit?**
Swipe left on the habit in your list and tap the delete icon.

**2. How do I restore my purchase?**
Go to Settings within the app and tap "Restore Purchases."

**3. Is my data private?**
Yes. We do not sell your personal data. Your chats with the AI are used solely for the app's functionality.
